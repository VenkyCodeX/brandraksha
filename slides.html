BrandProtect AI

A Digital Risk Protection platform for detecting fake social profiles, impersonating mobile apps, and lookalike domains — built around a verified brand identity.

Social Monitoring App Store Monitoring Domain Analysis Brand Profile

The Problem

Brands are impersonated
across every digital surface

  • Fake social profiles steal customers and damage trust
  • Scam accounts impersonate support to harvest credentials
  • Lookalike apps on app stores distribute malware or phishing
  • Typosquat domains redirect users to fraudulent pages
  • Organizations have no unified view of their external exposure
🎯

Challenge Scope

Brand Profile · Social Media Monitoring · App Store Monitoring · Lookalike Name Detection

⚡

Our Approach

Deterministic heuristics with explainable signals — no black-box ML, every score is traceable to a reason.
